# Privacy Policy: Shelfie

The full, current policy is on the website: **https://cmtania.github.io/bookshelf-tracker-docs/privacy.html**. The app and App Store Connect link to that page; this file is only a summary.

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

Shelfie does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Your library stays on your device.** Your books, categories, reading sessions, notes and settings are stored only on your iPhone. There are no servers and no accounts.
- **No analytics or tracking.** Shelfie contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".
- **Notifications are local.** Your iPhone schedules the reading reminders itself. No data is sent anywhere to deliver them.
- **Purchases are handled by Apple.** Shelfie Pro (the one-time Lifetime purchase and the Monthly subscription) is bought through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple Account.

If you delete the app, or use Settings → Reset all data, its data is deleted.
