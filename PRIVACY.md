# Privacy Policy — Shelfie

_Last updated: 26 September 2026_

Shelfie does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Your library stays on your device.** Your books, categories, reading sessions, notes and settings are stored only on your iPhone. We have no servers, and Shelfie has no accounts.
- **No analytics or tracking.** Shelfie contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- **Notifications are local.** Your iPhone schedules the reading reminders itself. No data is sent anywhere to deliver them.
- **Purchases are handled by Apple.** The one-time Unlock purchase goes through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

If you delete the app, all of its data is deleted with it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/cmtania/bookshelf-tracker/issues.
